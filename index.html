Click "Preview Screen" to begin.

Important Notes:
  • This application creates a live preview on the canvas, combining your screen and camera.
  • The saved video file will be a recording of this canvas output (including the circular camera overlay).
  • The output format will be .webm.
  • Audio from both your screen (system audio) and camera (microphone audio) will be included. Depending on your browser and media player, these might appear as separate audio tracks or be mixed.
  • Performance can vary based on your system's capabilities due to real-time video processing.
  • Please grant permissions for screen sharing and camera/microphone access when prompted.
